## ⌨️ Keybinds & Shortcuts

Control the entire engine straight from your keyboard:

| :---: | :--- | :--- |

## 🚀 Installation & Setup

> **Supported Browsers:** Google Chrome, Brave, Microsoft Edge, Opera, Kiwi Browser (Android).

### 3-Step Quick Install:

1. **Download the Package:**
   - Grab the latest release archive directly from [Telegram Channel](https://t.me/ibrprojects/4).
   - Extract the `.zip` archive to any folder on your PC.

2. **Open Extension Manager:**
   - Open your browser and navigate to:
     ```text
     chrome://extensions/
     ```
   - Switch on the **Developer mode** toggle in the top right corner.

3. **Load the Extension:**
   - Click the **Load unpacked** button.
   - Select the extracted `IBRChessEngine` folder.
   - Open [Chess.com](https://www.chess.com) and start any game or analysis — the engine connects automatically! ♟️
